Join the group

We work on real transportation problems in Indian cities using econometrics, choice modelling and data. If that interests you, there are several ways to get involved.

  • PhD positions

    For students with a master's degree in transportation engineering, urban planning, civil engineering or a related field, and an interest in quantitative modelling.

    Admission is through IIT Bombay's PhD admission process. Email me before applying to discuss research fit.

    IIT Bombay admissions portal

  • Postdoctoral fellows

    For recent PhDs in transportation, econometrics, operations research or related areas who want to lead projects that connect research and practice.

    Apply through the Institute Postdoctoral Fellowship scheme, and email me your CV and a short research statement.

    Apply for a postdoc at IIT Bombay

  • Internships

    For graduates with a bachelor's degree in civil engineering, transportation engineering, computer science, urban planning, traffic engineering or road safety who want hands-on research experience.

    Send your CV and a few lines on your interests.

    Email your CV

  • Industry and agency collaboration

    Consulting, sponsored research and training for transport agencies, transit operators, consultancies and companies.

    Engagements are contracted through IIT Bombay.

    See areas of expertise

What we look for

Curiosity about how people travel, comfort with mathematics and statistics, and willingness to learn programming in R or Python. Prior experience with choice models, GIS or traffic data helps but is not required.

Read a few of our recent papers and mention in your email which one interests you and why.
