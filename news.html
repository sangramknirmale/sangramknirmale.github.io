News and events

Grants, workshops, courses and conference presentations from the group.

  • 19-21 August 2026 Workshop

    3 Day CEP Workshop at IIT Bombay

    Successfully completed our 3-day short course on “Applied Econometric Methods for Empirical Transportation Research: Travel Behavior, Risk Analysis, and Policy Applications” at IIT Bombay. It was an enriching three days of lectures, hands-on sessions, discussions, and interactions with participants from India and abroad, representing universities, research and policy institutions, management institutions, and industry. Special thanks to Prof. Chiang Fu, National Cheng Kung University, Taiwan, for joining us as guest faculty and sharing his expertise and insights with the participants.

    IIT Bombay, Mumbai

  • 6–10 Jul 2026Conference

    Group presents at WCTR 2026 in Toulouse

    PhD students Meet Tabiyar, Biplab Sekhar Swain and Aayush Kumar Verma presented their research at the World Conference on Transport Research (WCTR), held in Toulouse, France.

    Toulouse, France

  • 9–11 Jul 2025Course

    Short course on transportation systems and demand modelling

    A three-day continuing education (CEP) short course, “Transportation Systems and Demand Modelling: Theory and Practice”, was held in the Department of Civil Engineering.

    IIT Bombay, Mumbai

  • 5 Apr 2025Grant

    Prime Minister Early Career Research Grant from ANRF

    Prof. Sangram Krishna Nirmale received the Prime Minister Early Career Research Grant from the Anusandhan National Research Foundation (ANRF), Government of India.

    IIT Bombay, Mumbai

  • 5 Feb 2025Workshop

    Workshop on OpenPaths AGENT

    The group organised a workshop on OpenPaths AGENT, with a session by Gaurav Vyas, Product Manager at Bentley Systems.

    IIT Bombay, Mumbai

  • 18 Nov 2024Workshop

    International workshop on travel demand modelling with emerging data

    The group organised an international workshop on Advancing Travel Demand Modeling Using Emerging Data Sources, sponsored by the Institution of Eminence, IIT Bombay. Sessions were led by Prof. Prateek Bansal (NUS Singapore), Prof. Amit Agrawal (IIT Roorkee) and Prof. Arnab Jana (IIT Bombay).

    IIT Bombay, Mumbai
